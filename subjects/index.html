Algebra

Solving a quadratic equation

Any equation ax² + bx + c = 0 can be solved with one formula. The discriminant tells you the kind of roots before you solve.

Formula

x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) / 2a D = b² − 4ac Example: x² − 5x + 6 = 0 ⟶ D = 1 ⟶ x = (5 ± 1)/2 = 3 or 2

Step by step

  1. 1Write the equation in standard form ax² + bx + c = 0 and read off a, b, c.
  2. 2Find D = b² − 4ac.
  3. 3D > 0: two real roots. D = 0: two equal roots. D < 0: no real roots.
  4. 4Put the values in the formula and simplify both signs (+ and −).

Rule to remember

Sum of roots = −b/a and product of roots = c/a. Use them to check your answer.

Why it matters in exams

Nature of roots and word problems come every year in Class 10 boards.
