02About

Concepts first. The marks follow.

Maths becomes easy when you understand the why, not just the how.

Ranjeet Choudhary Sir started teaching with one goal: to show students that Maths is not about memorising formulas but about understanding why they work. Over the past 8+ years he has taught Class 10, 11 and 12 Mathematics for CBSE Boards and JEE Main.

He holds a B.Sc., a B.Ed. and an M.Sc. in Mathematics. In class he builds every chapter from the basics, solves examples step by step on the board, and gives graded practice so that students can handle any question with confidence.

04Method

How a chapter is taught

A 6-step method that turns formulas and long problems into clear, logical steps.

  1. 01

    First-Principles Foundation

    Every formula is derived or explained before it is used, so students know where it comes from.

  2. 02

    Visual Understanding

    Graphs, number lines and diagrams make abstract ideas like functions, limits and vectors easy to see.

  3. 03

    Solved Examples First

    Each method is shown on a worked example before students try it themselves.

  4. 04

    Graded Practice

    From NCERT and exemplar questions to board sample papers and JEE Main level problems.

  5. 05

    Mistake Analysis & Doubts

    Regular test reviews to catch sign errors, calculation slips and common traps.

  6. 06

    Board Answer Writing

    How to present 3-mark and 5-mark answers step by step so no marks are lost.
