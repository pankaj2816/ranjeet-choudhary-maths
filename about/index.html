01Teaching Journey

Making Maths Clear Through Logic and Practice

Ranjeet Choudhary Sir started teaching with one goal: to show students that Maths is not about memorising formulas but about understanding why they work. Over the past 8+ years he has taught Class 10, 11 and 12 Mathematics for CBSE Boards and JEE Main.

He holds a B.Sc., a B.Ed. and an M.Sc. in Mathematics. In class he builds every chapter from the basics, solves examples step by step on the board, and gives graded practice so that students can handle any question with confidence.

02Teaching Philosophy

Maths becomes easy when you understand the why, not just the how.

The 6-step method followed in every batch and doubt class.

  1. 01

    Build Strong Basics

    Start every chapter from definitions and simple cases before moving to harder problems.

  2. 02

    See the Idea

    Use graphs, diagrams and number lines so that each concept makes visual sense.

  3. 03

    Learn from Solved Examples

    Work through model examples on the board, explaining the reason for every step.

  4. 04

    Graded Practice

    Practise from NCERT and exemplar questions up to board sample papers and JEE Main problems.

  5. 05

    Detailed Mistake Analysis

    Regular unit test reviews to catch sign errors, calculation slips and common traps.

  6. 06

    Exam Temperament

    Time management, neat step-wise presentation for board answers, and speed tactics for MCQs.
